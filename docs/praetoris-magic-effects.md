# Regular magic effect balance

Effect Duration and Trinket Duration increase duration by the percentages below.
Trinket Adrenaline Requirement reduces the requirement by the same percentages.

| Rarity | Minimum | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Magic | 3% | 5% |
| Rare | 5% | 7% |
| Epic | 7% | 10% |
| Legendary | 10% | 15% |
| Mythic | 15% | 20% |
| Ancient | 20% | 25% |

All three use 1-percentage-point roll increments. Their selection weights are
3 for Effect Duration and 1 for each trinket effect. These are relative weights,
not drop percentages. Total adrenaline reduction is capped at 50%.

Effect Duration supports staves, bows, crossbows, armor, utility items, and
trinkets. Trinket Duration requires a trinket with a full-adrenaline status effect.
Trinket Adrenaline Requirement is limited to trinkets.

These values apply to new rolls. Existing saved enchantments keep their values.
Shardstone balance is listed in [the shardstone documentation](praetoris-shardstones.md).
